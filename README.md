# Deutsch Word Builder Lab — The Double Life

Standalone offline lesson generated from the uploaded German transcript and the uploaded Deutsch Word Builder Lab specification.

## Open
Double-click `index.html`. No server, framework, package manager, or network connection is required for the lesson itself. German text-to-speech uses the browser/OS speech voices.

## Included
- 16-section learning flow
- cleaned full German story + Arabic and English translations
- 10 core vocabulary items
- article, compound, prefix, suffix, word-building, grammar and sentence-building practice
- direct-speech dialogues extracted from the story
- flashcards and 3 interactive practice modes
- 20 applied MCQs with explanations covering the correct answer and all distractors
- dark/light theme, Arabic RTL/English LTR, focus mode, search, progress and German TTS
- separate Anki `.apkg` deck in the parent delivery folder

## Source handling
Timestamps and recognition punctuation noise were removed. The final channel call-to-action was excluded from the lesson. The narrative events were not expanded with outside facts. Study examples and grammar prompts are pedagogical derivatives of the story.
